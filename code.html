 IMPORTANT DIRECTIONS FOR CANDIDATES
  1. Candidates must follow the instructions mentioned in this admit card.
  2. Candidates should report to the exam centre at least 120 minutes before the exam begins. No candidates will be allowed to enter the examination center after 9:30 am in Morning Shift and 2:30 pm in afternoon shift.
  3. Bring your own blue/black ballpoint pen to write particulars if needed.
  4. Without a valid admit card and original photo ID, entry is prohibited.
  5. No candidate shall leave the exam hall before conclusion and signing the attendance sheet.
  6. Follow the instructions mentioned in Notification strictly.
  7. No change of option of Language is allowed during examination. The questions from serial no. 91 to 120 pertain to Language 1 and questions from serial no. 120 to 150 pertain to Language 2.   11. Visit the exam centre a day before to confirm the location and transport.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR PERSONS WITH BENCHMARK DISABILITIES who have mentioned category PwD 2(R) or 2(s) in their online application form for CTET February - 2026:
  1. The provision of scribe can be allowed only on production of a certificate declaring physical limitation to write, issued by Govt. Health Care Institution as per Appendix-V of Notification before centre superintendent at examination centre on exam day.
  2. The scribe will be provided to the candidate at the examination centre by centre superintendent whose qualification would be one step below the qualification of the post for which candidate is appearing in examination.
  3. The candidates, eligible to avail the facility of scribe, will be allowed to meet the scribe for a 20-minute interaction session one hour before the start of the examination at the assigned examination centre.
  4. As per office memorandum F. No. 29-6/2019-DD-III dated 10th August 2022 issued by Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan), compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour of examination shall be allowed solely to those persons who are eligible for getting scribe. Therefore, such candidates should produce the certificate declaring physical limitation to write, issued by Govt. Health Care Institution as per Appendix – V of notification before centre superintendent at examination centre on exam day.
